Privacy policy

Private Household Assistant · last updated October 2026

This app is a personal tool used by a single household. It is not a commercial service and is not available to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): to summarise and search the owner's own email on request. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar: to read the owner's events, and to add events to the owner's own calendar only after the owner approves each one.

How data is handled

  • Google data is processed on the owner's own computer. Login tokens are stored only there.
  • Email content is processed only by AI models running on that computer and is never sent to an outside AI service.
  • Calendar details may be processed by an AI model service the owner chooses, only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • Short activity logs are kept on the owner's computer for 30 days and do not include email content.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
